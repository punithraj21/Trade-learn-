# Pullback Strategy

> **In one line:** Wait for a rising stock to pause and dip a little, then buy at the better price — instead of chasing it high.

## 🎯 What you'll learn
- What a pullback (a short dip inside a bigger up move) is.
- The 5 simple rules to trade a pullback in an uptrend.
- How to tell a healthy pullback from a real reversal (a full change of direction).
- Why this gives better risk-reward (reward compared to risk) than chasing.

## 📘 Key concepts

### What is a pullback?
A price does not go up in a straight line. It steps up, rests, steps up again.
- **Trend** — the main direction of price. An **uptrend** makes higher highs and higher lows.
- **Pullback (or retracement)** — a small, temporary dip against the trend.
- Think of a shopper walking up a hill. Now and then they stop to catch breath. The rest is the pullback. The climb is still on.
- Chasing means buying after a big jump. You pay top price. A pullback lets you buy on the pause — cheaper, with a closer safety net.

### The 5 rules (uptrend — buying the dip)
Follow them in order. Skip none.
1. **Confirm a clear uptrend.** Price makes higher highs and higher lows. No trend, no trade.
2. **Wait for the dip to a support zone.** A **support zone** is a price area where buyers stepped in before. This can also be a **moving average** (the average price of the last N days, a line the stock keeps bouncing off) or a **Fibonacci level** (common dip depths of 38.2% to 61.8% of the last up-move).
3. **Enter on a reversal trigger.** Do not buy while it is still falling. Wait for a **bullish candle** (a green bar showing buyers took control) or an **engulfing candle** (a green bar that fully covers the last red bar). This shows buyers are back.
4. **Stop below the pullback low.** A **stop-loss** is an auto-exit that caps your loss. Place it just under the lowest point of the dip.
5. **Set a target.** Aim for the prior high or beyond. Or **trail** your stop (move it up as price rises) to ride the trend.

### Healthy pullback vs reversal
This is the most important part. Buy a dip, not a falling knife.
- **Healthy pullback** — shallow, slow, orderly. It stops at support. The higher-low structure holds. Safe to consider.
- **Reversal** — deep, fast, ugly. It slices through support and makes a **lower low** (a bottom below the last bottom). The uptrend is broken. Stay out.
- Simple rule: if the dip breaks the last low, it is not a pullback anymore.

## 🔍 Example
A stock is in a clear uptrend.
- It rises from ₹100 to ₹120. Then it pauses and dips.
- It falls back to ₹110 — the old support zone. A green engulfing candle appears. Buyers are back.
- You **enter at ₹110**.
- You place your **stop-loss at ₹108** (just below the dip low). Risk = ₹2.
- You **target ₹130** (the prior high, plus a little). Reward = ₹20.
- Risk-reward = ₹2 risked to make ₹20. That is far better than buying at ₹120.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- **Buying while price is still dropping.** Wait for the green reversal candle first.
- **Trading a pullback with no real trend.** In a flat or falling market this fails.
- **Ignoring a broken low.** A lower low means reversal — that is not your setup.
- **Stop-loss too tight or missing.** No stop = one dip can wipe out your money.
- **As a beginner, going short.** Selling the bounce in a downtrend is the reverse of this. It is harder and riskier — stay long-only until experienced.

![The premature-entry mistake: buying while price is still falling, before it keeps dropping further and only later prints the reversal candle you should have waited for.](/diagrams/pullback-strategy-7.svg)

## ✅ Key takeaways
- Buy the dip inside an uptrend, not a random fall.
- Wait for a reversal candle at support before you enter.
- Stop goes below the pullback low; target the prior high or trail.
- Healthy pullback = shallow and orderly. Broken low = reversal, so skip it.
- Pullbacks give better risk-reward than chasing a breakout.

## 📝 Quick check
1. **Q:** Should you buy the moment price starts dipping?
   **A:** No. Wait for a bullish reversal candle at a support zone.
2. **Q:** The dip breaks below the last low. Pullback or reversal?
   **A:** Reversal. The uptrend is broken — stay out.
3. **Q:** Where do you place the stop-loss in a pullback buy?
   **A:** Just below the low of the pullback.

## 📖 New words
- **Trend** — the main direction price is moving.
- **Uptrend** — higher highs and higher lows.
- **Pullback (retracement)** — a small, temporary dip against the trend.
- **Support zone** — a price area where buyers have stepped in before.
- **Moving average** — a line of the average price over recent days.
- **Fibonacci level** — common dip depths (38.2%–61.8%) of a prior move.
- **Bullish candle** — a green bar showing buyers in control.
- **Engulfing candle** — a green bar that fully covers the prior red bar.
- **Stop-loss** — an auto-exit that caps your loss.
- **Lower low** — a bottom below the previous bottom, a reversal sign.
- **Risk-reward** — how much you may gain compared to what you risk.
- **Trail (trailing stop)** — moving your stop up as price rises.
